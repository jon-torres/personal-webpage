About Me

Hi, I'm Jon — a Software Engineer with a PhD in Linguistics and a passion for bridging the gap between problem solving and AI powered solutions.

I specialize in Natural Language Processing and Software Engineering, with hands-on experience across the full lifecycle of LLM-powered
pipelines — from ideation to deployment— by applying industry-standard LLMOps practices such as prompt engineering, model evaluation, monitoring, and continuous improvement.

My work spans both B2B and B2C contexts, helping organizations leverage AI to automate tasks like unit test generation and business process optimization.
Whether it's designing chatbots, developing LLM-powered pipelines, or architecting scalable systems, I bring a unique perspective that combines my multidisciplinary background.

Beyond code, you'll find me cooking up new recipes or being entertained by my playful black kitten — both activities that keep creativity flowing.
